MyTube

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

In short: MyTube is a personal, browser-based app. The app does not send your settings or viewing preferences to a MyTube server. If you choose Google sign-in, your browser communicates directly with Google and YouTube. The static site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and some page resources and images are loaded from third parties.

1. Who operates MyTube?

MyTube is a free, open-source project maintained primarily for personal use. It is a static website; there is no MyTube account system, application backend, or MyTube database. The project maintainer can be contacted through the GitHub profile. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.

2. Information handled by the app

When you use the demo feed, the app displays sample channel and video information. If you choose to connect a Google account, the app requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.readonly to retrieve your YouTube subscriptions and recent public uploads from those channels.
  • userinfo.profile and userinfo.email to show your Google name, profile picture, and email address in the interface.

The app requests subscription details (up to 100 channels), up to six recent uploads per channel, and video metadata such as title, thumbnail, publication date, and duration. It does not request permission to change your YouTube account or upload, edit, or delete videos.

3. Where information is stored

In your browser: MyTube stores app settings, your Google client ID or API key if you enter one, favorite and muted channel IDs, watched video IDs, and a cache of video IDs and durations in local storage.

In memory: While the page is open, Google profile information, the temporary OAuth access token, and data fetched from YouTube are held in the app's memory. The app does not save the access token or Google profile to its own persistent storage. If you previously authorized the app, it may ask Google for a fresh token silently on a later visit.

Browser storage is specific to the browser and device you use. MyTube does not receive a copy of this information. The Google API key field is optional; the current app stores its value with your settings but does not use it in its API requests. Exporting settings downloads a JSON file to your device; that file may contain your preferences and configured IDs or keys.

Data in local storage remains until you clear this site's data in your browser or remove it using your browser's storage controls. The app has no in-app delete-all control. Deleting the app or closing a tab does not necessarily clear local storage. Exported backup files must be deleted separately.

MyTube does not encrypt browser storage itself. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to access locally stored settings and exported backup files. Protect your device and avoid using a shared browser profile for account configuration.

4. Google, YouTube, and embedded videos

The Google Identity Services script is loaded from Google when you visit the app, including when you use the demo feed. When you sign in, Google handles the sign-in and authorization flow. MyTube then sends requests directly from your browser to Google's user information and YouTube Data API endpoints, using the temporary access token. Google and YouTube may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser/device details, the requested resources, and authorization data. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to their processing.

Playing a video loads an embedded player from youtube-nocookie.com. The player may communicate with YouTube and receive technical information about your browser and the video being played. The privacy-enhanced domain does not mean that no data is processed; YouTube's terms and privacy policy apply to the player.

If you choose to open a video directly on YouTube, your browser connects to YouTube's website and is then subject to YouTube's standard privacy practices.

The app also loads images from YouTube's image servers and, in the demo feed, from Unsplash. Your browser connects to those services to display the images. Review the applicable Google Privacy Policy, YouTube privacy information, and Unsplash Privacy Policy.

If Google returns a profile-picture URL, your browser loads that image from Google's servers when your profile is displayed.

5. Hosting and technical requests

The site is published as a static site using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser data, to deliver and secure the site. This processing is controlled by GitHub under its own terms and privacy statement; MyTube does not receive GitHub's hosting logs. See GitHub Pages information and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

6. Cookies, analytics, and advertising

MyTube does not itself set cookies or use analytics or advertising services. This does not prevent Google, YouTube, GitHub, or image providers from processing data or using technologies under their own policies when your browser contacts their services, including during sign-in or video playback.

7. Purposes and retention

The app uses the requested information only to show your profile, subscriptions, recent uploads, and video information, and to apply your local preferences. Google account access is initiated by you and managed through Google's consent screen. On later visits, the app attempts to restore an existing Google authorization without storing the access token; Google may require you to sign in or grant access again. Preferences and the duration cache remain in your browser until you clear them. Temporary app data and the access token are kept in memory while the page is open and are discarded when the page is unloaded. GitHub, Google, YouTube, and image providers may retain data under their own policies.

MyTube does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. See the Google API Services User Data Policy for Google's requirements concerning data obtained through its APIs.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

  • You can use the demo feed without signing in.
  • You can clear MyTube's local data in your browser's site-data settings. This also removes saved preferences and cached durations.
  • You can revoke MyTube's access in your Google Account permissions.
  • Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information. MyTube's maintainer does not hold your local app data; requests about information processed by Google, GitHub, YouTube, or Unsplash should be directed to the relevant provider.
  • Where applicable, you may also lodge a complaint with your competent data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children

MyTube is a general-purpose personal project and is not designed specifically for children. It does not ask for a user's age. Google sign-in and YouTube use are subject to the relevant provider's age requirements and terms.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data flows change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. The current source code is available in the MyTube repository.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub profile linked above. The maintainer has no access to information stored only in your browser. For privacy requests, use a private contact method listed on the profile, if available; do not submit personal data in a public issue.

MyTube is a personal, open-source project and is not a Google or YouTube product. This policy describes the app's current implementation and is not legal advice.
